Market Superiority

Why Japanese Parts Beat Overseas Sourcing

Two structural advantages make Japan the world’s undisputed automotive parts capital:

Strict Japanese Standard

1. The "Shaken" Inspection System

Japan enforces mandatory, high-cost bi-annual vehicle inspections (Shaken). Because keeping older cars certified is expensive, Japanese drivers maintain vehicles meticulously, retiring or trading them in early at low mileage.

  • Meticulously Maintained: Strict dealer service logs & OEM maintenance.
  • Low Mileage: Average Japanese scrap car has far fewer miles than US/EU equivalents.
  • Immaculate Used Parts: Clean, rust-free components with high service life remaining.
Currency Advantage

2. Historic Weak Yen (JPY) Pricing

The ongoing depreciation of the Japanese Yen grants North American and European businesses historically unmatched purchasing power.

  • Direct Japan Wholesale: Eliminate 2-to-3 tiers of domestic importer markups.
  • 30%–50% Cost Advantage: Substantial savings on genuine OEM parts and rare JDM assemblies.
  • Pay in USD / EUR / GBP: Transparent currency conversion without hidden fees.
Dual Inventory Access

3. New OEM & Inspected Used

One single point of contact for factory-sealed genuine components and vetted pre-owned parts from Japan’s licensed dismantler networks.

  • Brand New OEM: Direct supply from Toyota, Nissan, Honda & Subaru distribution.
  • Physical Photo Portfolios: Used parts photographed from multiple angles prior to invoicing.
  • Discontinued Sourcing: Access to rare heritage stock that local dealers list as discontinued.
